Cookie Policy

Last updated: May 5, 2026

This page lists every cookie that chipsoffury.com and app.chipsoffury.com may set in your browser, what each one does, and how long it lasts.

You can change your analytics choice at any time using the Cookie Settings link in our footer.

Strictly necessary

These cookies are required for the site and app to function. They do not require consent.

Name Purpose Lifetime
cof_consent Stores your cookie-consent choice (Accept / Reject) so we don't show the banner again. 1 year
popup-dismissed Suppresses the "open in mobile app" popup on app.chipsoffury.com after you dismiss it once. Session
Auth tokens Keep you signed in between visits to app.chipsoffury.com. Up to 30 days

Analytics (optional)

These cookies are only set if you click Accept on our cookie banner. They help us understand which pages and features are useful so we can improve them. We do not sell or share this data.

Name Set by Purpose Lifetime
_ga Google Analytics Distinguishes unique visitors. 2 years
_ga_<id> Google Analytics Persists session state for a specific GA4 property. 2 years

If you reject analytics, Google Analytics still receives cookieless pings (no _ga cookie, no persistent identifier) so we retain a basic understanding of overall traffic without tracking individuals. This is Google's Advanced Consent Mode.
